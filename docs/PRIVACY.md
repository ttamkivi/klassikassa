# Privacy

A class directory holds personal data of families other than yours: children's names,
parents' names, emails and phones, bank account numbers, and who paid what. You hold it as
a volunteer on the class's behalf. Treat it that way.

- **Local only.** The directory lives on your computer. Keep it out of git repositories,
  cloud-synced folders you share, and chats. If you use git for the code, add the class
  directory to `.gitignore` before you put any data in it.
- **Least data.** The roster needs a child's name and a way to reach the parent. Do not
  add what you do not need (personal codes, addresses, notes about a family's situation).
- **Each family sees its own status.** Reports to parents show totals and counts, never
  names of families who have not paid. Reminders are one family at a time.
- **Shared payment tables need the class's agreement.** Some classes prefer a table
  everyone can see. That is fine when the class has been asked (opt-out is the usual way,
  in writing, before the first shared table) and `transparency = "open"` is set.
- **AI assistants.** If you use one, it reads the class data. Use one whose data handling
  you accept for other people's information, keep the data in your own session, and do not
  paste the roster into services that train on your input.
- **Hand-over.** When you pass the role on, hand over the directory in person or through a
  channel you would trust with bank details, then delete your copy of the contacts.
- **End of school.** When the class graduates, keep the report and the ledger totals; delete
  the contact list.
